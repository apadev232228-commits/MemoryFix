# Privacy Policy for MemoryFix

**Last Updated:** October 2026

MemoryFix ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your data.

---

### 1. 100% On-Device & Offline Processing
- **Your Photos, Videos, and Memories Never Leave Your Device.**
- MemoryFix processes, parses, repairs EXIF timestamps, embeds GPS coordinates, stitches multi-snap videos, and merges captions/stickers **strictly locally on your device**.
- We operate **no backend servers or cloud storage** that ingest or receive your personal media files.

---

### 2. Permissions & Data Access
- **Storage / Storage Access Framework (SAF):**
  Used exclusively to read the Snapchat ZIP archive or folder you explicitly choose, and to save the repaired photos and videos to your device's Pictures/Gallery folder (`Pictures/Snapchat Memories`).
- **Foreground Service (`dataSync`):**
  Used to keep processing active in the background when fixing large archives, ensuring the operating system does not kill the task prematurely while you use other apps.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):**
  Used solely to display progress updates while an archive is being processed.

---

### 3. Optional Diagnostics & Feedback
- If you encounter an error or choose to submit feedback via the "Feedback & Support" screen, you may optionally attach an encrypted diagnostic summary.
- This diagnostic log includes:
  - Device model, Android OS version, and free storage space.
  - Scan counts (photos fixed, videos fixed, errors encountered).
  - Internal execution log lines to troubleshoot file parsing failures.
- **Diagnostics are never uploaded automatically.** They are only shared if you explicitly tap "Send Feedback Report" through your email client.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
MemoryFix integrates with standard Google Play services to support the app:
- **Google Play Billing:** Processes Pro lifetime purchases securely. We never store or have access to your credit card or financial credentials.
- **Google AdMob:** Serves ads to non-Pro users. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers (GAID) in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.
- **Google Play Core (In-App Review & In-App Updates):** Enables checking for app updates and submitting Google Play ratings.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
MemoryFix does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
