Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for MemoryFix

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

🔒

100% Offline & On-Device Processing

Your photos, videos, and Snapchat archive files never leave your device. MemoryFix operates no cloud servers and cannot access, upload, or view your personal memories.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

MemoryFix is built with privacy-first principles. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal media:

  • Photos & Videos: Read directly from your chosen export archive and saved to your device's photo gallery (Pictures/Snapchat Memories). Never transmitted anywhere.
  • Timestamps & GPS Metadata: Extracted strictly from local JSON indexes on your phone to update EXIF tags.
  • Personal Information: No account creation, login, name, phone number, or email registration is required.

2. Permissions Requested & Their Purpose

MemoryFix only requests essential permissions necessary to perform user-initiated file restoration:

  • Storage (Storage Access Framework): Allows you to select your Snapchat data ZIP or folder and write restored files to your local photo gallery. No broad external storage access is requested.
  • Foreground Service (Data Sync): Keeps restoration active in the background when processing large archives so Android does not interrupt the process when you switch apps or turn off your screen.
  • Notifications: Shows real-time progress while fixing your archive and notifies you when the restoration completes.

3. Diagnostics & Feedback

If you encounter an issue and choose to contact developer support via the in-app "Feedback & Support" screen:

  • You may optionally choose to attach a diagnostic summary.
  • This log includes device technical specifications (e.g., device model, Android OS version, storage available) and error lines to help troubleshoot why a file failed.
  • Diagnostics are never uploaded automatically. They are only shared if you explicitly choose to attach them via your email client.

4. Third-Party Services

MemoryFix integrates with standard Google Play developer libraries to support app functionality:

  • Google Play Billing: Securely processes in-app Lifetime Pro purchases. We never see or store your payment details or credit cards.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Serves non-intrusive ads to free users. AdMob may collect device identifiers in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Core: Checks for official app updates and facilitates Google Play store reviews.

5. Children's Privacy

MemoryFix does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. All file operations occur strictly offline on the user's local hardware.
